All FAQs →
Can you help with my design?

Yes. Share your logo, artwork or idea and we’ll help develop it for your packaging. Your quote confirms the design help included, and you approve a digital proof before printing.

When will my packaging arrive?

Printed coffee cups are delivered in under a month from artwork approval. Other lines take several weeks. We confirm timing with your quote and keep you updated.

Are online prices final?

They are indicative. Your written quote confirms pricing. VAT is added at 20%.

How do payment and delivery work?

A 50% deposit starts production and the balance is due before delivery. Delivery to one UK mainland address is free.

What print and lining options are there?

One to four print colours, depending on the product. Hot cups come with recyclable PE or compostable PLA linings.
