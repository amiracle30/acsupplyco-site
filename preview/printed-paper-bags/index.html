Good questions

Before your first order.

Ask us something else ↗
Can I use my own design?

Yes. Share your logo or print-ready artwork when requesting a quote. We will confirm the artwork requirements for your chosen bag, and you approve a digital proof before anything is printed.

What if my artwork is not ready?

You can request a quote first and share your artwork later. Tell us what you have in mind and we can discuss the next steps.

Are the prices shown final?

The prices on this page are indicative and exclude VAT, which is added at 20%. Your digital proof and print plates are included, and delivery is free to a single UK mainland address. We confirm everything in a written quote before you commit.

Which bag style should I choose?

Twisted-handle takeaway bags suit hot food and meal orders. Flat-handle carriers are the classic retail and bakery bag, with the wide-gusset version for larger or boxed items. SOS grab bags have no handles and suit pastries, sandwiches and counter service. If you are unsure, tell us what goes in the bag and we will advise.

Do you print other bag sizes?

Yes — small and large versions of most styles are available. Use “Request a custom quantity”, tell us the size you need, and we will quote it.

When will my bags arrive?

As a general guide, allow several weeks from order confirmation to delivery. We will confirm current timing with your quote.
