Ask us something else ↗
Can I use my own design?

Yes. Share your logo or print-ready artwork when requesting a quote. We will confirm the artwork requirements for your chosen item, and you approve a digital proof before anything is printed.

What if my artwork is not ready?

You can request a quote first and share your artwork later. Tell us what you have in mind and we can discuss the next steps.

Are the prices shown final?

The prices on this page are indicative and exclude VAT, which is added at 20%. Your digital proof and print plates are included, and delivery is free to a single UK mainland address. We confirm everything in a written quote before you commit.

Do you print larger single wall cups?

Yes — 8, 12 and 16 oz single wall cups are available on request. For most cafés we recommend our double wall cups, which are priced online.

When will my cups arrive?

Printed espresso cups are delivered in 4 weeks from artwork approval. We will confirm current availability and delivery timing with your quote.
