Ask us something else ↗
Can I use my own design?

Yes. Share your logo or print-ready artwork when requesting a quote. We will confirm the artwork requirements for your chosen box, and you approve a digital proof before anything is printed.

What if my artwork is not ready?

You can request a quote first and share your artwork later. Tell us what you have in mind and we can discuss the next steps.

Are the prices shown final?

The prices on this page are indicative and exclude VAT, which is added at 20%. Your digital proof and print plates are included, and delivery is free to a single UK mainland address. We confirm everything in a written quote before you commit.

Which size do I need?

Standard is 120 × 120 × 70 mm and Large is 135 × 135 × 70 mm. If you are unsure, tell us your bun size and build height in the enquiry and we will advise.

Can the boxes be laminated?

Gloss and matte lamination are available and are quoted on request. Select the finish you want and send us the configuration.

When will my boxes arrive?

As a general guide, allow several weeks from artwork approval to delivery. We will confirm current timing with your quote.
